### 💡 Tips for generating the best App Icons:
1. **Aspect Ratio**: Always generate in a 1:1 square ratio.
2. **Simplicity**: The best app icons have a single, recognizable focal point. Don't add text (like the word "OnWay") into the icon itself — it gets too small to read on a phone screen.
3. **Contrast**: Ensure the white logo pops heavily against the Green/Amber backgrounds.
4. **Consistency**: Use the same style (e.g., minimalist vector) for both prompts so the apps look like they belong to the same family.
